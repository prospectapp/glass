Glass Agenda

Glass Agenda does not collect, store or share any personal information.

Glass Agenda reads events from the calendars on your Mac to show them, and never connects to the internet.

No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.

Glass arXiv

Glass arXiv does not collect, store or share any personal information.

Network. Glass arXiv downloads new paper listings from arXiv's public API. Only your chosen categories are sent; nothing about you.

Your settings (categories, keywords) and saved papers stay on your Mac.

No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.

Glass Ask

Glass Ask does not collect, store or share any personal information.

Glass Ask uses Apple's on-device language model. Your text is processed on your Mac and never sent anywhere. Your recent answers are kept on your Mac until you clear them.

No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.

Glass Aurora

Glass Aurora does not collect, store or share any personal information.

Glass Aurora downloads public space-weather data from NOAA; nothing about you is sent with that. Your location is used on your Mac to read the forecast for where you are. To show the name of the place, or to find a place you type, it asks Apple's geocoding service: your location, or the name you typed, is sent to Apple for that and to no one else.

No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.

Glass Awake

Glass Awake does not collect, store or share any personal information.

Your settings (your usual duration and options) are kept on your Mac.

Downloads folder (only if you choose "Until downloads finish"): Glass Awake looks at the names of files there to see whether any are still downloading. It never opens, reads or sends them.

Network. Glass Awake doesn't connect to the internet.

No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.

No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.

Glass Bar

Glass Bar does not collect, store or share any personal information.

Your settings (how soon icons hide again, and whether the shortcut is on) are kept on your Mac. On macOS 27 and later, the version from outside the App Store also keeps, on your Mac only, which apps you chose to hide and the names of apps that have been open (so their icons can stay).

Microphone and camera. On macOS 27 and later, that version asks macOS whether a microphone or camera is in use, so it can show every icon while one is. It never listens or looks, and needs no permission.

Network. Glass Bar doesn't connect to the internet.

No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.

Glass Battery Health

Glass Battery Health does not collect, store or share any personal information.

Battery readings. The app reads your Mac's battery capacity, charge cycles and temperature from macOS and keeps one reading a day, in its own storage on your Mac, so it can show how your battery changes over time. This history never leaves your Mac unless you export it yourself.

Network. The app doesn't connect to the internet.

Notifications (optional) are created on your Mac.

No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.

Glass Citations

Glass Citations does not collect, store or share any personal information.

What you look up. The name or ORCID iD you enter is sent to the service you choose, to find your papers and the papers that cite them: OpenAlex (api.openalex.org) for every field, or NASA ADS (api.adsabs.harvard.edu) for astronomy. Nothing else about you is sent.

Keys and tokens. An ADS token, or an OpenAlex key if you add one, is kept in your Mac’s keychain and sent only to the service it belongs to.

Your papers and citations are kept on your Mac so the app can tell what’s new.

Network. Glass Citations connects only to the service you choose: OpenAlex or NASA ADS.

No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.

Glass EQ

Glass EQ does not collect, store or share any personal information.

Audio. To apply your headphone EQ, Glass EQ processes your Mac's sound as it plays, on your Mac. macOS asks your permission for this (System Audio Recording). The sound is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Mac speakers. In Automatic mode, Glass EQ checks which apps are playing sound (their names only, through macOS's audio system) to choose a speaker sound. This stays on your Mac and isn't stored.

Network. Glass EQ doesn't connect to the internet. Its headphone data is included in the app.

Your settings and profiles (chosen profile, Bass and Treble, color, EQ profiles you add) stay on your Mac, inside Glass EQ's own storage.

No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.

Glass Sky

Glass Sky does not collect, store or share any personal information.

Location. With your permission, Glass Sky uses your location to work out the sky where you are. It's kept on your Mac. To get the cloud forecast, the location is sent to Apple's weather service (WeatherKit); to show the name of the place, or to find a city you type, it's sent to Apple's geocoding service. It goes to no one else.

Network. Glass Sky downloads the cloud forecast (Apple Weather) and the Space Station's orbit (CelesTrak). Nothing about you is sent with them.

Your settings (place, alerts) stay on your Mac.

No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
